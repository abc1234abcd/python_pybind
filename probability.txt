central limit theorem
1. normal distribution comes from CLT. same i.i.d random variable, x_1, x_2,..., x_n has mean miu and variance sigma**2
if yi = 1/sqrt(n)*sum(xi-miu), then the distribution of y convergence to that of normal distribution(0, sigma**2)
therefore, P(y_n <= x) -> P(normal(0, sigma**2)<= x)
